📥 Menu Importer

One-time tool: bring your Sheet menu into Supabase

🔐 Staff Login

This tool writes directly to your menu_items table, so it's staff-only.

1. Paste your menu CSV

Open your Google Sheet → File → Share → Publish to web → CSV, then paste the published CSV URL below and click Fetch, or copy/paste the raw CSV data directly into the box.

2. Map your columns

Skip any field your sheet doesn't have. Rows missing a required ID or Name will be skipped.

3. Preview & Import
